RULE 1 • PERMISSIBLE THRESHOLD
BF ∈ {−1, 0, +1}

Heights differ by at most one level. Re-compute node height h = 1 + max(hL, hR) and return.

RULE 2 • CRITICAL DISRUPTION
|BF| ≥ 2 Trigger

Balance invariant violated. Identify child sign and dispatch one of the 4 standard rotations immediately.

When to pick AVL?

Whenever reads outnumber writes by more than 2:1. The stricter height bound minimizes cache misses.

Space Footprint

Stores a 2-bit balance factor or 1-byte height value per node, fitting easily within pointer alignment padding.

Guaranteed Bounds

Maximum 1 single or double rotation per insertion restores the entire tree's balance condition.
